02 — What you get

Server features

SMP + Economy

Survival with a real economy — player shops, an auction house, loot crates and a spawn-stash that keeps your items safe.

Duels & FFA

PvP when you want it — 1v1 duels and free-for-all arenas, kept separate from peaceful survival.

Crossplay

Java and Bedrock players in the same world — compatible from 1.21.11 up to the latest 26.3 thanks to ViaVersion.

Voice Chat

Proximity voice chat is on — talk to friends like you're really standing next to them in the world.

Custom Touches

Custom villager trades, custom enchantments, emotes, random teleports and other little extras.

Manually Moderated

No auto-anticheat bans — a real human staff reviews every report, so disputes get fair, human eyes before anything happens.
